MONEY PLAN — CLOUDFLARE PUSH SETUP

1. In Cloudflare Workers & Pages > money-plan-notifications > Edit code, replace the entire worker.js contents with the code in worker.js from this ZIP. Click Deploy.
2. Keep your existing KV binding named PUSH_KV and the hourly Cron Trigger: 0 * * * *.
3. Keep these Worker settings: VAPID_PRIVATE_KEY (Secret), VAPID_PUBLIC_KEY (Text), VAPID_SUBJECT (Text). Never share your private key.
4. Visit https://money-plan-notifications.oobayoopa.workers.dev/health and check for {"ok":true,"storage":true}.
5. Upload the updated app files (index.html, sw.js, manifest, icons, wallpapers) from this ZIP to your existing GitHub Pages repo, NOT worker.js. Do not upload the push-server folder to GitHub.
6. Open Money Plan on Android over HTTPS. Under reminders, the Worker URL is prefilled. Tap Enable background push, then Allow notifications. The app will fetch the public key automatically.
7. Reminders are checked hourly and intended for UK time: Sunday 3pm, Wednesday 6pm, 1st of month 6pm. Delivery can be delayed; this is not guaranteed real-time.

If /health shows an error or subscriptions fail, inspect Cloudflare Worker logs. This implementation uses native WebCrypto and has not been live-tested against your deployed Cloudflare account. Do not delete your existing app or clear its storage; export a backup before replacing files.
